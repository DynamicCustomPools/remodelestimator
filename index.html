1. All work will be performed according to current National Plasterers Council Technical Standards.

2. Drain pool completely and punch appropriate number of hydrostatic holes in the bottom of pool to ensure structural integrity of pool during work.

3. Remove existing tile, coping and install new (If applicable).

4. Remove plaster around all returns, lights, rope anchors and main drain covers.

5. Pool will then be chipped, as necessary, to remove any loose plaster, hollow spots or paint.

6. Acid wash entire surface with muriatic acid to etch the surface and neutralize acid with soda ash.

7. Apply Multi-Kote, a bonding agent that ensures that the new plaster binds to existing surfaces. Allow to dry.

8. Two coats of plaster will be applied. Plaster will be hand troweled.

9. The water is started immediately for curing purposes — Do NOT turn off water until water reaches the middle of waterline tile. It is the customer's responsibility to turn water off.

10. Special care will be taken to protect homeowner's property during the work, and all clean-up will be performed in a timely manner.

11. Recommended literature from The National Plasterers Council will be given to customer for proper start up and filling procedures, if prior arrangements for startup procedure are not arranged.
